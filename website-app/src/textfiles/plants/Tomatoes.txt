This is the progress of my homegrown cherry tomatoes! I moved them inside my apartment oncd the weather dropped to 14 degrees and they have been thriving inside. I have probably harvested around 50 tomatoes and some are sweet like candy! Nothing better than homegrown tomatoes!


They did grow a bit tall because I didn't date prune them properly. I did chop them in half twice and kept the tops to grow more, and boy did they grow!
It took a lot of effort to grow them. During the hottest days of the summer, I had to water them every 5 hours so they didn't dry out on the balcony. But I still managed, even though I travelled a bunch this summer. Thankfully, I have kind friends who were up for the task of watering the tomatoes when I was gone.

Hindsight, the amount of tomatoes I got compared against the amount of time and money I've spent on it was not worth it in any other way other than getting the satisfaction of accomplishing to grow something edible. Don't get me wrong, it was super fun! But I did not do it to save money.

Anyhow, I'm gonna grow my own tomatoes next year too. Maybe I dare try to grow my own cucumber too.
