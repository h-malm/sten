Finally, I can see signs of fall. It's getting cold in the night and the leaves are turning yellow. The nights are getting longer as well, which means it's officially candle-season.

I don't think I'll ever understand the strong dislike for fall and winter. While I agree that the rain can be bothersome, I know that the reason I don't like rain is because where I live, it is never just rainy. The wind is a bitch and never lets up. If it were just rain, it'd be so cozy, but trying to go somewhere when the wind throws the rain up in your face even when you have an umbrella or cap is incredibly annoying.

Back to the positives of fall. Darkness is the best part along with the colors of the trees. The darkness brings a comfort for me. Also, my eyes are sensitive to the sun so in the summer I'm constantly having to shield my eyes even when I have sunglasses and a cap. It gets old real quick. Going for walks as the sun is setting and the air gets noticeable colder brings me a sense of appreciation for nature. It feels natural for the environment to cool down, for the fog to roll in over the fields, and the darkness letting the animals go to sleep.

Maybe I like it because I'm born in fall and I remember it as spending time with family and friends, celebrating my, my sister's, and my friend's birthdays. My sister's birthday is a couple days before mine and my friend's birthday is one day before mine. Either way, I am convinced that the reason I like fall is because of the darkness and the possibility of sitting and reading or hanging out in candle-light.
